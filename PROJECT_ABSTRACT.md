# PROJECT ABSTRACT

## 📌 Project Title
**KGiSL Smart Academic & Automated Near-Ultrasonic Attendance System**

---

## 👨‍💻 Project Overview
In modern educational institutions, traditional roll-call and paper-based attendance marking are inherently slow, error-prone, and susceptible to proxy attendance. Furthermore, students often lack real-time visibility into their cumulative attendance percentages and exam eligibility thresholds.

To overcome these challenges, the **KGiSL Smart Academic & Automated Attendance System** introduces a state-of-the-art, web-based campus management platform powered by **Near-Ultrasonic Acoustic Broadcast Technology (18.2 kHz - 19.7 kHz)**, **Dynamic Anti-Proxy QR Code Verification**, **Automated Attendance Calculators**, and an **Autonomous Role-Aware AI Agent**.

Designed specifically for the MCA Department at **KGiSL Institute of Information Management**, this application seamlessly unifies Students, Faculty Members, and Department Administrators into a single, high-performance digital ecosystem.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules & System Innovations

### 1. Dual-Technology Zero-Proxy Attendance Engine
- **Alpha Acoustic Broadcast (18.2 kHz – 19.7 kHz)**: Transmits encoded digital tokens via near-ultrasonic high-frequency sound waves from faculty laptops. Operating in a near-ultrasonic band, the sound is **100% silent to human ears** while being effortlessly captured by student smartphone microphones up to **15 meters across a lecture hall**.
- **Beta Dynamic QR Fallback**: Displays dynamic, time-decaying QR codes on classroom displays with location verification to ensure students are physically present in the assigned room.

### 2. Smart Student Portal & Attendance Advisor
- **Safe Bunk & Mandatory Attendance Calculator**: Automatically analyzes attendance history and advises students on how many classes they can safely skip while maintaining the mandatory 75% university eligibility criteria, or how many consecutive classes they must attend if facing a shortage.
- **Live Daily Schedule**: Displays period-by-period class timetables with live pulsing indicators for active periods.
- **College Academic Calendar**: Interactive calendar tracking semester holidays, college events, and Block Test exam schedules with built-in document converter utilities.

### 3. Official 1-Click PDF & CSV Exporters
- **A4 PDF Report Generator**: Produces official college attendance sheets complete with KGiSL institutional headers, department seals, and HOD/Faculty signature lines formatted for university inspections (NAAC/NBA audits).
- **Shortage Defaulters List (< 75%)**: Instantly filters and exports warning lists for students falling below the 75% threshold for immediate notice board or parental notification.

### 4. Autonomous Full-Database AI Agent ("Genius AI Copilot")
- **Fuzzy Tanglish NLP Engine**: Decodes natural language commands typed in English or informal Tanglish (e.g., *"my today sessions"*, *"chitra m ai"*, *"aug 1 to aug 5 report download"*).
- **Custom Date Range Filter**: Dynamically parses user-specified date ranges and generates on-demand CSV attendance sheets directly inside the chat window.
- **Autonomous Task Execution**: Executes live database actions such as reviewing leave requests or assigning faculty timetable sessions upon authorized command.

### 5. Multi-Role Authorization & Security System
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Enforces strict data separation and permission boundaries across Students, Faculty, and Department Administrators.
- **Secure Authentication**: Protected with JSON Web Tokens (JWT), refresh token rotation, and BCrypt password hashing to ensure institutional data integrity and privacy.
